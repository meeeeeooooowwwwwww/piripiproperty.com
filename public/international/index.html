New Zealand property from overseas

Exploring New Zealand property while living abroad? Whether you already own property here or are considering an investment, understanding the local rules and the right next steps matters. This is an introduction to making an enquiry with Corrie Piripi, who is based in Christchurch.

Overseas investors: understand eligibility first

New Zealand restricts many overseas purchases of residential property. From 6 March 2026, a specific pathway allows certain qualifying Active Investor Plus, Investor 1 and Investor 2 visa holders to apply for consent to buy or build qualifying residential property above NZ$5 million. This is not permission for overseas buyers generally, and property, consent and visa conditions apply.

Before making plans, review Land Information New Zealand's investor-visa guidance and obtain independent legal and immigration advice about your circumstances.

* Official reference: LINZ investor-visa rules.

Already own property in New Zealand?

Owners who live overseas may have questions about a potential sale, local market context, communicating across time zones or finding the appropriate NZ professionals. An initial enquiry can establish the location, purpose and questions involved without sharing sensitive documents.

Property enquiries outside Christchurch

If your property interests are elsewhere in New Zealand, include the region and general property type when you get in touch. Any availability, representation, specialist involvement or travel would need to be discussed and confirmed before an engagement could be considered.

Start with a conversation

For an initial enquiry, tell us whether you are a potential buyer, existing owner or authorised representative, where the property is generally located and what you hope to achieve. Please do not send passport details, bank records, identity documents or visa information in a website enquiry.
